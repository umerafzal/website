## Overview

A personal project exploring how AI and messaging tools could reduce admin work for driving instructors.

## Status

**Idea / early exploration** — features below are design targets, not shipped functionality unless stated otherwise.

## Problem

Instructors juggle students, lessons, breaks, holidays, and reminders across disconnected tools.

## Planned directions

- Student management and lesson history (concept)
- Calendar integration for availability and bookings (concept)
- WhatsApp-oriented communication patterns (concept)
- Voice messages and morning briefings (concept)
- Automated reminders (concept)

## Technologies (intended)

AI orchestration, TypeScript, calendar APIs, messaging integrations — exact stack to be decided as the prototype evolves.
