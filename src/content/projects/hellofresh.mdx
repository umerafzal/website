## Overview

Berlin-based senior iOS role on HelloFresh’s mobile products — combining native iOS delivery with React Native surfaces where the product is shared across brands.

## Role

Senior iOS Engineer working with cross-functional partners on feature definition, implementation, and sustainable engineering practices.

## Context

Large consumer mobile organisation with multiple app experiences and shared product goals across regions and brands.

## Problem

Referral and growth-related flows must stay consistent and maintainable while native and React Native codebases evolve in parallel.

## Approach

- Deliver referral-related capabilities on iOS with clear requirements and testable outcomes.
- Contribute to React Native work for sibling brands (e.g. Factor, YouFoodz) to reach feature parity where product asks for aligned referral behaviour — without duplicating business logic unnecessarily.
- Uphold code quality, documentation, and review standards on the team.

## Engineering challenges

- Keeping parity and quality across native and RN stacks.
- Balancing product deadlines with structural improvements in a large codebase.

## My contribution

Hands-on iOS and React Native implementation, collaboration with product and UX, and engineering practices that support a maintainable codebase — described here at a level appropriate for a public portfolio.

## Technologies

iOS, Swift, React Native, TypeScript (as used on RN surfaces), modular mobile architecture.

## Lessons learned

Cross-platform product alignment works best when boundaries, ownership, and parity goals are explicit early.
