## Overview

iOS Engineer on foodpanda’s consumer application in Singapore — focused on wallet and digital payments experiences on a high-traffic marketplace app.

## Role

iOS Engineer within payments-oriented mobile workstreams, partnering with product and design on user-facing flows.

## Context

Consumer super-app patterns where wallet, checkout, and trust signals must feel dependable under real-world network and device conditions.

## Problem

Wallet access and payment flows need strong UX, appropriate security layers, and code that teams can extend without fragile coupling.

## Approach

- Ship wallet-related iOS features with attention to edge cases and user-visible error handling.
- Introduce biometric authentication as an additional layer for accessing wallet features where product and platform guidelines allow.
- Deliver platform capabilities such as home-screen widgets (iOS 14+) that support the product roadmap.
- Improve test coverage and module structure in wallet-related code over time.

## Engineering challenges

- Security and UX tradeoffs on device.
- Maintaining quality in a large, fast-moving iOS codebase.

## My contribution

Feature implementation, architectural discussions, and quality improvements on wallet-related modules — summarised here without internal operational detail.

## Technologies

iOS, Swift, consumer wallet / payments patterns, WidgetKit (era-appropriate).

## Lessons learned

Payments-adjacent mobile work rewards explicit state modelling and early alignment on failure modes users actually see.
