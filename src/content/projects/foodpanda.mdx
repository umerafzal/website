## Overview

Senior iOS role on foodpanda’s consumer application — feature delivery, architecture discussions, and quality bar for production mobile software.

## Role

Senior iOS Engineer focused on iOS surfaces and mobile architecture practices.

## Context

Fast-moving consumer marketplace with demanding performance and reliability expectations on mobile.

## Problem

Wallet and payments-adjacent flows on mobile require careful UX, error handling, and coordination with platform APIs — without compromising security or maintainability.

## Approach

Iterative delivery with strong code review culture, reusable patterns for common mobile concerns, and architecture that teams could extend as the app grew.

## Engineering challenges

- Balancing product velocity with structural improvements.
- Wallet-related flows that must feel dependable to users under real network conditions.

## My contribution

iOS implementation and technical leadership on assigned domains, collaboration with cross-functional partners, and contributions to shared mobile standards.

## Technologies

iOS, Swift, consumer mobile patterns, wallet-related mobile experiences (described at a high level).

## Lessons learned

Consumer wallet experiences fail loudly when edge cases are treated as rare — investing in clarity early saves incident time later.
